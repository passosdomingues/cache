Data Usage Agreement

This interactive career navigator is based on data from the Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025.

⚠️ Important Notice

The Stack Overflow survey data represents trends from a specific developer community and should not be considered statistically representative of the entire global developer population. Recommendations are based on aggregated trends and should serve as directional guidance rather than absolute career advice.

Privacy & Data Handling

  • No personal data is stored or transmitted to external servers

  • Your responses and results remain completely private

How Recommendations Are Calculated

Our algorithm analyzes your response spectrum across behavioral traits, technical skills, and values. Each dimension is weighted and matched against market trends from the Stack Overflow data. The system uses a hybrid filtering approach combining your profile attributes with collaborative trends from developers with similar characteristics.
